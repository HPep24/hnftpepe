HNFT PEPE

A hobby artist whose work lives on chain, most of it on Bitcoin.

Cards issued on Counterparty. Vectors written whole into Bitcoin’s UTXO set. Inscriptions on individual sats. Code that runs from witness data on ACME. And field work on Tezos and Base.

Much of it is small on purpose. Compressionism, as the UTXO room puts it, treats reduction as craft and constraint as catalyst: art tight enough to be stored in full, with nothing kept anywhere else.

This site is the catalogue. Each room shows its work in its own way and keeps a still wall beside it. Every piece opens a card with its on-chain facts and the links to view it, collect it and verify it; the index lists all of them in one place.
